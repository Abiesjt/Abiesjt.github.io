Motorized 3D-Printed Orthosis for Hand Rehabilitation

Research Paper

Designed and developed a low-cost, 3D-printed hand exoskeleton to assist people experiencing hand immobility due to neuromuscular conditions. Worked in a team of four to integrate EMG sensors with an Arduino so detected muscle contractions could control motor functions. The project received an A+.

Arduino-Based Breathalyzer for Blood Alcohol Estimation

Designed and calibrated a low-cost breathalyzer using the MQ-3 alcohol sensor and Arduino to estimate blood alcohol concentration (BAC) from breath alcohol concentration (BrAC) readings. Calibrated sensor readings and implemented a voltage-divider circuit with a Wheatstone bridge to improve measurement accuracy.

Machine Learning Model for Kidney Cancer Detection

Developed a machine learning model using more than 10,000 CT and PET scans to classify kidney images as normal or tumour cases, achieving 92% reported accuracy. Applied generative AI techniques and data augmentation to increase dataset diversity and improve classification.
