A low-cost breathalyzer prototype built around an MQ-3 alcohol sensor and Arduino, translating breath-alcohol concentration (BrAC) readings into an estimated blood-alcohol concentration (BAC).

01 / HARDWARE

Integrated the MQ-3 sensor with an Arduino-based measurement system and a voltage-divider / Wheatstone-bridge circuit.

02 / CALIBRATION

Calibrated sensor readings to improve measurement consistency and support the BrAC-to-BAC calculation.
